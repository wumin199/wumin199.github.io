    Inspiration is perishable and life goes by fast. Inaction is a particularly insidious type of risk.<br>
    灵感易逝，人生苦短。躺平，是一种特别被忽视的风险。<br><br>
    Spend more time recruiting. Take risks on high-potential people with a fast rate of improvement. Look for evidence of getting stuff done in addition to intelligence.<br>
    把更多时间用在招聘上。提拔那些进步快、潜力大的人。看人不要只看是不是聪明，关键是能不能成事。
